Professional Biography

Decades of Defining Legal Precedent

Eleanor Sterling took silk in 2012 following an illustrious career as a junior advocate specializing in commercial chancery and corporate disputes. Her practice centers on advising FTSE 100 corporations, sovereign wealth funds, and ultra-high-net-worth individuals navigating critical enterprise risks.

Recognized consistently by Chambers & Partners and The Legal 500 as a Star Individual, Eleanor is noted for her forensic intellect, devastating cross-examination style, and immaculate strategic foresight in settlement negotiations. She has acted as lead counsel in over seventy reported appellate decisions in the UK and overseas common law jurisdictions.

"Ms. Sterling commands the courtroom with an authoritative grace and intellectual precision that completely alters the dynamics of complex commercial litigation."

— Chambers UK Bar Guide, Global Elite Practitioner Review
